Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025  ·  Effective immediately upon use of our services

Your privacy matters. Zenith is designed to collect the minimum data necessary to operate. We do not sell your data, and we do not store any data beyond your active session unless explicitly noted below.

1. Who We Are

Zenith ("we", "our", "the Service") is a Discord bot and web dashboard for Discord server management. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use Zenith.

2. What Data We Collect

When you log in via Discord OAuth, we temporarily store the following in your session:

  • Discord User ID — a unique identifier for your Discord account
  • Username and display name — as shown on your Discord profile
  • Avatar URL — your Discord profile picture
  • OAuth access token — used to fetch your server list; stored in-memory for your session only
  • List of Discord servers — retrieved on-demand to show servers you manage; never stored persistently

We do not collect, store, or access:

  • Your messages or direct messages
  • Server message history
  • Payment or financial information
  • Email address or phone number
  • Any data beyond what is listed above

3. How We Use Your Data

Data is used solely to authenticate you, display servers you manage, and identify your server in the dashboard. We do not use your data for advertising, analytics profiling, or any other purpose.

4. Data Retention

Session data is held in server memory only — no persistent database. Sessions expire after 7 days of inactivity or immediately on logout. When your session ends, all data is cleared.

5. Cookies

We use a single session cookie (connect.sid) to maintain your login. It is HttpOnly, Secure (HTTPS only), and SameSite: Lax. We do not use tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies.

6. Discord Bot Data

When Zenith is active in your server, the bot processes interactions to execute commands. It does not log or store message content. Any bot-generated records (strikes, LOAs, etc.) exist within your Discord server and are subject to Discord's data practices. This section will be updated as bot features expand.

7. Third Parties

We rely on Discord for authentication. We do not share your data with any other third parties.

8. Data Security

We use HTTPS, HttpOnly cookies, and in-memory-only sessions. No system is completely secure — we encourage you to log out when you are done.

9. Your Rights

Since we hold no persistent database, there is no stored profile to delete or export. Log out at any time to immediately clear all session data. For concerns, contact us using the information below.

10. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13 and will remove any such data if identified.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Changes are effective when posted. Continued use constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Reach out to the Zenith team. Contact details will be provided once the team directory is finalized.
